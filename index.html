02 / Terms

The terms, in full.

These terms, together with the Deal Schedule above, are the whole agreement for the engagement.

1

Parties

This Agreement is between Kamani Brown, sole proprietor, of Dieppe, New Brunswick, Canada (“Editor”), and the client named in the Deal Schedule (“Client”). The Editor provides short-form video editing and content services to creators, agencies, and brands.

2

Deal Schedule

The services, price, and start date are set by the package the Client selects in the Deal Schedule:

  • Paid Test Edit — $100 one-time. One Short / Reel / TikTok edited from Client-supplied footage, 48-hour turnaround, full production quality (hook framing, captions, sound design, pacing, end card). The $100 is credited in full toward the first month if the Client signs a package within 7 days of delivery. The test fee is payable on delivery of the finished test edit.
  • Shorts Engine — $1,150/month. 12 Shorts / Reels / TikToks per month from Client-supplied footage; captions, hooks, sound, retention pacing on every video; batched weekly delivery.
  • Shorts Engine Plus — $1,850/month. 20 Shorts / Reels / TikToks per month; thumbnails / cover frames for every video; titles and descriptions written in the Client’s voice; uploading and scheduling to YouTube / Instagram / TikTok; a monthly performance snapshot.
  • Content Partner — $2,600/month (maximum 2 clients). 12 Shorts + 2 long-form YouTube edits per month; thumbnails, titles, descriptions, scheduling; monthly strategy call and content calendar planning; priority 24–48 hour turnaround on Shorts.

Add-ons (quoted and agreed before work starts; never billed as a surprise): extra Short edit $95; thumbnail only $30; rush (24-hour) +$50 per video; extra revision round $50; community management block (capped 5 hrs/week) +$400/month.

Anything outside the selected package is out of scope until added in writing (email is enough).

3

Term and cancellation

Month-to-month. Either side may end the Agreement with 14 days’ written notice (email is enough). Work already paid for is delivered; the slot is released at the end of the notice period. The Paid Test Edit is a one-time engagement and carries no ongoing term.

4

Fees and payment

Month 1: 50% of the monthly fee is payable upfront to reserve the slot; editing does not begin and the slot is not held until it clears. The balance of month 1 is due Net-15 from the start date.

Cancelling before month 1 work begins: if the Client cancels after paying the upfront amount, it is refundable minus a $150 administration hold when the slot has been held fewer than 7 days. Once the slot has been held 7 days or more, the upfront payment is non-refundable.

Months 2 onward: invoiced monthly in advance, due on receipt / before that month’s work begins.

Payment Plans Available: Wise (preferred), PayPal, or Payoneer, at the Client’s choice.

Net receipt: all fees are quoted in USD and are net to the Editor. Any transfer, conversion, or platform fees charged by the payment method the Client chooses are the Client’s responsibility (grossed up where needed), so the full invoiced amount clears to the Editor. A fee counts as paid only when the full amount has cleared in USD.

Late payment: overdue amounts carry a late fee of 2% per month. The Editor may pause work on an account more than 7 days overdue; paused time does not extend the month or create a credit.

5

Deliverables, turnaround, revisions

Standard turnaround is 48–72 hours per Short from receipt of complete footage and brief (Content Partner Shorts: priority 24–48 hours). Delivery is batched (weekly for Shorts Engine) unless otherwise agreed.

Each video includes 2 revision rounds. A revision round is one consolidated list of changes per video. Further rounds are an add-on at $50 per round.

Turnaround clocks run on business days and start when footage, brief, and any brand assets are all in hand — not when the first file arrives.

Late-work credit: if the Editor misses the agreed turnaround for a full week for reasons within the Editor’s control, the Client receives a pro-rated credit for that week against the next invoice. Delays caused by late footage, assets, or feedback do not create a credit.

6

Client responsibilities

The Client supplies raw footage, briefs, brand assets, logins (where uploading/scheduling is included), and timely feedback.

The Client warrants it owns or has licensed all rights in the footage, music, images, logos, and other materials it supplies, and that the Editor’s use of them as instructed will not infringe any third party’s rights. The Client indemnifies the Editor against claims arising from Client-supplied materials.

Delays caused by late footage, assets, or feedback do not reduce the monthly fee and do not roll unused video slots into the next month.

7

White-label and confidentiality

Where the Client is an agency or resells the work, the Editor works fully white-label: the Editor’s name, branding, and identity do not appear in or on the delivered work, and the agency’s clients will not be told the Editor exists.

The Editor will not solicit, pitch, or accept work directly from the agency’s clients introduced through this engagement, during the term and for 6 months after it ends.

Each side keeps the other’s non-public information (client lists, rates, strategies, credentials) confidential and uses it only to perform this Agreement. This clause survives the end of the Agreement.

8

Ownership and portfolio use

Finished, delivered videos become the Client’s property on full payment of all fees owing for them, including the right to publish, monetise, and edit them further.

Raw project files (edit timelines, project files, and the working assets used to create the videos) remain the Editor’s property. Footage and materials the Client supplied remain the Client’s property. A buyout of the Editor’s project files and working assets is available at a standard $300 per package-month, agreed in writing.

Unless the engagement is white-label or under NDA, the Editor may show delivered work in his portfolio and case studies. A Client may opt out of portfolio use in writing at any time, effective going forward.

Until full payment clears, delivered previews may be watermarked and no licence to publish is granted.

9

Pause policy

The Client may request one pause of up to 14 days per quarter, with 7 days’ notice, for genuine production breaks (no footage available). The billing cycle shifts by the paused days; it is not a discount.

Pauses beyond that, or pauses requested without notice, do not shift billing — the slot is being held.

The Editor may pause for illness, emergency, or a late-payment hold under clause 4, with notice as early as practical; Editor-initiated pauses shift the cycle day-for-day.

10

Liability

Each side’s total liability under this Agreement is capped at the fees paid or payable in the month the claim arose. Neither side is liable for indirect or consequential losses (lost revenue, lost followers, demonetisation) except where caused by wilful misconduct. Nothing in this clause limits the Client’s obligation to pay for work delivered.

11

Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of New Brunswick, Canada. Disputes will be raised in writing first and negotiated in good faith for 14 days before either side starts court proceedings in New Brunswick.

12

Whole agreement and changes

This document plus the Deal Schedule is the whole agreement for the engagement. Changes must be in writing (email is enough). If the Client’s own paperwork (purchase orders, vendor terms) conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement governs unless Kamani Brown agrees otherwise in a signed writing.

13

Signatures

Signed electronically or by hand on the returned PDF. The signed copy returned to the Editor is the binding record. Signature blocks are below, and the same blocks appear in the PDF saved in Step 2. Deal Schedule attached: package selected, monthly fee, start date, payment method chosen.
